A great story deserves a place you want to stay.

That’s the idea behind the latest update I’ve been building for Talerooms.

I wanted the experience to feel more like settling into a good book: thoughtful typography, a calmer layout, and a reading room you can make your own.

Here’s what’s new:

• A refreshed visual identity and cleaner bookshelves.
• Paper, Sepia, Night, and OLED reading themes.
• Adjustable fonts, text size, and line spacing—with your preferences remembered on your browser.
• Focus mode for those “just one more chapter” moments.
• Chapter position and estimated reading time, plus a fix to saved-page restoration.

My favourite part is how these small choices add up. Reading at night, reading on your phone, picking up where you left off—those moments deserve as much care as the first impression.

I’m building Talerooms into a place where stories find their people. This update is another step toward that.
